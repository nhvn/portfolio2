## Intro

Task Master is a task management app built around a simple idea: most to-do apps are either too basic or way too complicated. We wanted something in between - simple enough to actually use daily, but with enough structure to keep both daily tasks and longer-term goals from falling through the cracks.

## Problem

Sticky notes get lost, most task apps are overkill, and it's easy to lose track of what actually matters versus what's just noise on a list.

## Solution

Task Master splits things into "Today," "Agenda," and "Objectives" boxes, plus a Bulletin Board for quick notes, so daily tasks and bigger goals each have their own space instead of getting mixed into one long list.

## Early Concepts

We started simple - the goal was a tool people could actually stick with, not one more app that gets abandoned after a week.

### Early Ideation

The "Today," "Agenda," and "Objectives" split came out of wanting to separate tasks by urgency and time frame instead of dumping everything into one list. The Bulletin Board came later as a spot for the random notes that don't fit anywhere else.
