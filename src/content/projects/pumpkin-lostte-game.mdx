## Overview

Pumpkin Lostte is my Halloween-themed twist on Flappy Bird - dodge obstacles as Jerry to rescue his lost pumpkin. Unlike Flappy Bird, there's an actual finish line instead of just playing until you die.

## Sketches

Early on I focused on the core chase mechanic - the player is being pursued the whole time, which adds urgency on top of the usual dodge-and-weave gameplay. The sketches below are from that stage.

## Methodology

I built this iteratively, starting with a basic game loop and layering on barrier navigation and game-over conditions from there. I hand-designed Jerry, the Pumpkin, the Chaser, and the black barriers - the Chaser and barriers went through a lot of iteration, mostly sketched out on my notes app between builds.

## Results

The finish-line twist is what sets this apart from other endless dodgers, and the Halloween theme ended up being a fun bonus on top of that.

## Final Thoughts

This was my first milestone project from my bootcamp - a browser game built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, themed around Halloween since that's when I built it. Looking back, there's plenty I'd do differently now, but for where my skills were at the time, I'm still proud of it. Give it a shot if you're up for a challenge - it's not an easy game, so don't feel bad if it takes a few tries.
