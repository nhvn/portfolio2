## Intro

BeFit V2 came out of my own frustration with juggling separate apps for workouts, diet, and weight tracking. I wanted one place that actually tied it all together instead of a fitness tracker that only does one of those things well.

## Problem

Tracking workouts, food, and weight across different apps gets messy fast, and it's easy to lose the bigger picture of how you're actually doing.

## Solution

BeFit v2 puts workout logging, calorie tracking, and weight measurements in one app, so you can actually see how everything connects instead of piecing it together yourself.

## Research & Discovery

### User Research

We ran surveys and interviews with people who work out regularly to figure out what actually mattered to them, which shaped what we built first.

[Link to Survey](https://forms.gle/6VCiYpjxpUgyoQmy8)

### Color Schemes & Font

We went with high contrast and a clean look - black background with white text and accents in dark mode, white background with black text in light mode, blue and soft gray as accent colors. The narrative text uses a soft blue-gray that felt right for a wellness app.
