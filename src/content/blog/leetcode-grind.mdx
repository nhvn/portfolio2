I hadn't touched LeetCode in over a year. Between my internship and everything else going on, interview prep just fell off completely, and I didn't really notice how much until I sat down to actually practice again.

## Realizing how much I forgot

I opened a problem I would've solved without thinking twice a year ago and just blanked. Not on the concept, but on the actual patterns - when to reach for two pointers versus sliding window, when a problem is secretly a graph problem, how to even set up the DP table. It's not that I forgot how to code, it's that the pattern recognition you build up from doing this constantly just fades if you stop.

## Starting over, sort of

Rather than randomly picking problems again, I'm working through [NeetCode 150](https://neetcode.io/practice) to rebuild that foundation properly - arrays and hashing, two pointers, sliding window, stacks, all the way through graphs and DP. The hope is that going through it in order, grouped by pattern, gets me back to where I actually recognize a problem type instead of re-deriving it from scratch every time.

## Fitting it back into life

Between my Master's, job searching, and everything else, I don't have unlimited hours for this, so I'm trying to keep it consistent instead of cramming - a bit most days rather than a big session once a week. We'll see how it goes, but it already feels less painful than I expected getting back into it.
