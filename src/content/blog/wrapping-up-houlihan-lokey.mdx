My time as a [Data Engineer Intern at Houlihan Lokey](/#work) is wrapping up, and I've been sitting with that a lot more than I expected to. It's easy to think of an internship as a line on a resume, but this one turned into something I'll actually miss.

## The people I'll miss

The team here made this internship what it was. I got thrown into real data engineering problems from early on, and never once felt like I was just "the intern" - people took the time to explain the why behind decisions, not just the how, and were genuinely invested in me actually understanding the work. That's rarer than it sounds, and it's the part I'll miss the most.

## Getting me through the hard weeks

What I didn't expect going in was how much this role would help me get through some of the most stressful stretches of my Master's program. Between exams, assignments, and the internship all landing in the same weeks more than once, having a team that was understanding and steady made a real difference. It gave me something stable to hold onto when school felt like too much, and honestly, it got me through in more ways than I can fully put into words here.

## Still learning, even now

Even this close to wrapping up, I'm still learning - that's maybe the part that surprised me most. Watching how my peers approached problems, picked up on things I'd missed, and explained their reasoning taught me as much as any of the actual project work did. It's a good reminder that a lot of the best learning doesn't come from a task at all - it comes from the people sitting next to you doing the work with you.

## Grateful

I'm walking away from this internship with a lot more than experience on paper. Thank you to everyone on the team - this one's going to stick with me.
