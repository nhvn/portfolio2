My time as a [Data Engineer Intern at Houlihan Lokey](/#work) is wrapping up, and it's been sitting with me more than I expected. It's easy to think of an internship as a line on a resume, but this one turned into something I'll actually miss.

## The people I'll miss

The team here made this internship what it was. I got thrown into real data engineering problems early on and never once felt like just "the intern" - people took the time to explain the why behind decisions, not just the how, and actually cared whether I understood the work. That's rarer than it sounds, and it's what I'll miss most.

## Getting me through the hard weeks

What I didn't expect going in was how much this role would help me get through some of the hardest stretches of my Master's. More than once, exams, assignments, and work all landed in the same week, and having a team that was steady and understanding made a real difference. It gave me something to hold onto when school felt like too much.

## Still learning, even now

I'm still learning, even this close to done - that surprised me. Watching how my peers approached problems, picked up on things I'd missed, and explained their thinking taught me as much as any of the project work. A good reminder that a lot of the best learning doesn't come from the task, it comes from the people next to you doing it with you.

## Grateful

I'm walking away with a lot more than experience on paper. Thanks to everyone on the team - this one's going to stick with me.
