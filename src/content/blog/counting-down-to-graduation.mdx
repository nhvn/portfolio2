It's starting to feel real. My time in the [Master of Science in Computer Science program at USC](/#education) is winding down, and I've been catching myself reflecting on the path that got me here more than usual lately.

## The winding path

My route into this wasn't exactly a straight line - a Bachelor's in Natural Sciences at UC Irvine, then a full-stack development program at Cal State Long Beach that's really what pulled me into software, and now closing things out with a Master's at USC. None of it looked planned from the start, but every step ended up feeding directly into the next one.

## What actually stuck

A few things from this program are going to stick with me long after the degree is done:

- **Depth over breadth, eventually.** Early on I wanted to touch everything - data engineering, full-stack, AI. USC gave me the space to actually go deep in a few of those instead of staying surface-level in all of them.
- **Building things that ship.** Between coursework, my internship, and side projects, I got a lot more comfortable with the "actually finish and ship it" part of engineering, not just the "get it working on my machine" part.
- **The people.** Some of the best conversations I've had about this field happened in study rooms at 11pm before a deadline, not in lecture.

## What's next

With graduation right around the corner, I'm genuinely excited (and a little nervous, if I'm honest) about the next chapter - actively looking for full-time roles in software, data, or AI engineering. If you're working on something interesting and think I'd be a good fit, [I'd love to hear from you](/#contact).

For now though, I'm trying to actually sit with this moment instead of rushing past it. It's been a long few years to get here.
