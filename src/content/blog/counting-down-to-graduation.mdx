It's starting to feel real. My time in the [Master's program at USC](/#education) is winding down, and I've been thinking a lot about the path that got me here.

## The winding path

It wasn't a straight line. I started with a Bachelor's in Natural Sciences at UC Irvine, then did a full-stack program at Cal State Long Beach that's really what pulled me into software, and now I'm closing things out at USC. None of it was planned from the start, but each step somehow set up the next one.

## What stuck

A few things from this program I know I'll keep with me. I came in wanting to touch everything - data, full-stack, AI - and USC pushed me to actually go deep in a few of those instead of staying surface-level in all of them. Between classes, my internship, and side projects, I also got a lot more comfortable shipping things instead of just getting them running on my laptop. And some of the best conversations I've had about this field happened in a study room at 11pm the night before a deadline, not in lecture.

## What's next

I'm excited for what's next, a little nervous too. I'm actively looking for full-time roles in software, data, or AI engineering, so if you're working on something interesting and think I'd be a fit, [let's talk](/#contact).

For now, I'm trying to actually sit with this instead of rushing past it. It's been a long few years.
